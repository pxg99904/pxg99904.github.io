How to:

DIGITALLY ARCHIVE YOUR TRADITIONAL ARTWORK

From pencil sketches to canvas paintings, two easy methods to archive everything. Perfect for storage purposes or print-making.

PRELIMINARY STEPS:

a. Gather your artwork and assess which method will be best to archive the work.

We will cover the following here:

- Flat Artworks (Paper, Photographs, Etc.) — Scanning

- Oversized or Bulky Artworks — Photography

b. Create two folders on your computer or drop box, one for raw images and one for final processed images. Consider if you want subsections. For example: close ups of artwork, full image edited final copies, etc.

WHAT YOU’LL NEED:

1. Your Artwork

2. A scanner or Camera (Phone or DSLR, DSLR is preferred)

3. Tripod (If using camera)

4. Editing Software (Built into phone, or specialized programs such as Lightroom, Photoshop, etc.)

5. Helpful Tools: Gray card (Photography), external storage (hard drive preferred, flash drive is better than nothing)
