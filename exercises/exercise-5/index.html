Best and Worst Running Shoes (to me).

No one asked but I shall deliver. Unsolicited shoe advice may be the best shoe advice. Don't waste your money on bad shoes. Invest in things that seperate you from the ground; I.E: tires, bed, and shoes. Also go to your local running store and get fit because 85% of people wear shoes that are too small.

Asics

Novablast 5: Hurt me 1/10
Magic Speed 4: Best shoe ever perfect for race day 11/10

Saucony

Endorphin Azura: Chefs kiss. Amazing daily trainer. 12/10
Echelon 10: Good daily trainer for wide feet. Kinda lame tho. 6/10.

Brooks

Glycerin Max: Mileage eater but also a brick. Good for half marathon training though. 6.5/10.
Hyperion Max 3&4: Literally blister machines for me. Ran my fastest 5k in v3 though. 6/10.
